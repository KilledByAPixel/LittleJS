    Render3D

    LensFlare3D

    LensFlare3D - The sun's lens flare, the old kind: a glow at the sun and a row of discs and rings of different sizes along the line from the sun through the middle of the screen

    • Make one and it shows, over the 3D scene and under what the game draws after, a HUD; destroy it to take it away
    • It follows render3D.sunDirection, and fades out as the sun leaves the screen or goes behind something
    • flareSize, count, intensity and saturation set its look, seed picks another arrangement, and its color tints it, with the sun's own color; or give it elements of your own
    • visible is how much of the sun shows, 0 to 1, eased over fadeTime, there for a game to read
    • What hides the sun is found with a ray from the camera, against the level and every object that is not see through, each as the box around its mesh, see render3D.pick; turn it off with occlusion
    • It needs WebGL, and it draws nothing in the shadow of renderAfter2D

    Extends EngineObject3D

    Constructor

    new LensFlare3D(size, count, intensity, saturation, color)render3dExtras.js:1529

    Create the sun's lens flare

    size number optional, default 1

    Scales every part of it, 1 by default

    count number optional, default 7

    How many ghosts there are along the line, besides the glow at the sun

    intensity number optional, default 1

    How bright it is

    saturation number optional, default 1

    How colorful the ghosts are, 0 for all the flare's own color, 1 a rainbow

    color Color optional

    Tints the whole flare, with the sun's color

    new LensFlare3D;                // the sun flares
new LensFlare3D(1.5, 10, .7, 0); // bigger, 10 ghosts, dimmer, all one color
