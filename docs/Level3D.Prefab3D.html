    Prefab3D

    An instance of a prefab, what a prefab's type makes: a handle with no shape of its own, and its parts, what the prefab's objects made

    • Parts of a prefab that is not attached are objects of their own in the world: moving, turning or sizing the handle puts them where it now says with its next update, or call placeParts; destroying it destroys them
    • Parts of an attached prefab are its children and move with it as one body

    Extends EngineObject3D
