    Parallax

    ParallaxLayer

    A background layer with parallax: an image that follows the camera by a part of its movement and repeats across the view, drawn once into a canvas by a function, mountains when none is given

    • A far layer has a parallax near 1 and a low renderOrder, a near one a smaller parallax and a higher renderOrder
    • The image should meet itself at its left and right edges to repeat without a seam, as the mountains do

    Extends CanvasLayer

    Constructor

    new ParallaxLayer(pos, size, parallax, renderOrder, drawFunction, canvasSize)parallax.js:33

    Create a parallax layer and draw its image

    pos Vector2 optional

    Where the middle of the image is in the world when the camera is there too

    size Vector2 optional

    World size of one copy of the image

    parallax number|Vector2 optional, default 0.5

    How much of the camera's movement it follows, 0 stays with the world and 1 with the screen, a Vector2 to follow x and y by different amounts

    renderOrder number optional

    Low to draw behind the game, far layers lowest

    drawFunction function optional

    Draws the image, given the canvas context, its size in pixels and the layer; mountains when not given

    canvasSize Vector2 optional

    Size of the image in pixels

    // three ranges of mountains, the far ones lighter and slower
for (let i = 3; i--;)
    new ParallaxLayer(vec2(0, i*2), vec2(40, 20), .9 - i*.2, -1e3 + i,
        parallaxMountains(hsl(.6, .3, .7 - i*.2), hsl(.6, .5, .2), i));

// or draw your own image into the layer
new ParallaxLayer(vec2(), vec2(40, 20), .5, -1e3, (context, size)=>
{
    context.fillStyle = '#fff';
    context.fillRect(size.x/2 - 20, size.y/2 - 20, 40, 40);
});
