    Box2D Joint

    • Base class for Box2D joints
    • A joint is used to connect objects together
    • Angular values are clockwise like angle: joint angles and speeds, limits, motor speeds and torques

    Extended by Box2dDistanceJoint, Box2dFrictionJoint, Box2dGearJoint, Box2dMotorJoint, Box2dPrismaticJoint, Box2dPulleyJoint, Box2dRevoluteJoint, Box2dRopeJoint, Box2dTargetJoint, Box2dWeldJoint, Box2dWheelJoint
